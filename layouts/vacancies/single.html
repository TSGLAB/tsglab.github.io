Research Assistants in Continual and Automated Interpretability (2 posts)

Duration 6 months
Location Oxford, in person. Remote arrangements may be considered in exceptional cases.
Start date As soon as possible
Principal investigator Fazl Barez

AI systems can change after deployment through in-context learning, memory, continued training, or other forms of adaptation. Most interpretability methods, however, analyse a fixed model at a single point in time.

This project will develop tools for studying how internal representations and circuits change as a model learns from experience. We are interested in which features remain stable, how different forms of adaptation alter internal mechanisms, and whether changes in behaviour can be anticipated from changes inside the model. The aim is to automate this analysis with minimal manual annotation.

We are particularly interested in candidates with experience in one or more of the following areas:

  • Pre-training or post-training, particularly work involving internal model analysis
  • Continual learning, including plasticity loss, catastrophic forgetting, and task-boundary effects
  • Automated interpretability methods, including sparse autoencoders, activation patching, and circuit discovery
  • PyTorch or JAX, supported by strong machine-learning fundamentals

Experience in every area is not required. Candidates should be able to work independently, implement ideas carefully, and communicate research findings clearly.
