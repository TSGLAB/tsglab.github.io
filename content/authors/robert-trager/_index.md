Prof Robert Trager is a Principal Investigator at the Technical Safety & Governance Lab (TSG) and co-director of the AI Governance Initiative at the University of Oxford. His research examines how AI governance can be designed at institutional, national, and international levels to ensure safe and beneficial outcomes from advanced AI development.
